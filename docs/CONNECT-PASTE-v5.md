## Description
```
Lữ Đăng is an offline classroom for printed 四柱 teaching plates (教材 / 印本).

Open the plate. The hour pillar is blank (时辰未记). Thi asks you to pick 1 of 12 地支; the grade is khóa / lập luận — khớp khóa 教材 or lệch khóa — not a destiny score.

Hiệu khảo collates the on-device engine against a curated passage from 子平真诠, 渊海子平, 三命通会, 穷通宝鉴, or 滴天髓 (合 / 歧). Tap a cite to open that passage.

应期 marks recorded events on the same plate as classical rules that hold or do not hold.

The 1,523-text library is the citation court. BaZi is the calculator. Everything runs on the device.
```
