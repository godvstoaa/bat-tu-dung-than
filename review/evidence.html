3. The Classical Text Library 道藏 Thư viện kinh điển

Lữ Đăng embeds a curated corpus of 1,523 classical Daoist and metaphysical texts (approximately 3.14 million characters), spanning the full Daoist canon (道藏), medical classics (黄帝内经), metaphysics and feng shui literature. To our knowledge, no mobile application — on any platform — ships a comparable collection. The library is browsable in-app at battu.god8.shop/kinh/index.html.

3.1 Four analysis layers

Each text is accompanied by four layers of original scholarly work, generated by a dedicated content pipeline and reviewed for catalog accuracy:

LayerDescriptionCoverage
1 · Essence 提要A summary of the text's core teaching and its place in the tradition.100%
2 · Deep analysis 章節分析Chapter-by-chapter analysis of structure, doctrine and practice.90%
3 · Logic thesis 邏輯鏈An explicit reasoning chain linking the text's doctrine to practical interpretation.100%
4 · Full translation 越譯Complete Vietnamese translation of the source text.86%

3.2 Scholarly verification

All 765 catalog identifiers (DZ#) were cross-checked against the standard academic references for the Daoist canon: Schipper–Verellen Concordance of the Daoist Canon, Komjathy, Pregadio, ctext.org and kanripo.org. Corrections from the verification pass were applied programmatically and re-verified.

4. On-device Analysis Engines 計算引擎 Động cơ phân tích

Every analytical result in Lữ Đăng is computed locally by purpose-built calculation engines (180+ modules) implementing classical algorithms — no server API, no static lookup tables. Chart construction, element balancing and fortune cycles are deterministic and reproducible.

4.1 BaZi core engine

The central engine constructs a complete Four Pillars (四柱) chart from the user's birth data and produces a 40+ section, ~85 KB personalized analysis — covering chart structure (格局), governing element (用神), ten-god combinations (十神), element balance (五行), decade fortune cycles (大运), yearly forecasts (流年) and daily timing. The analysis is generated on-device and rendered in the app; a live example renders automatically at the link in the header.

4.2 Deep reference & schools

  • Classical rule base: 120/120 調候 rules from 穷通宝鉴, plus hundreds of rules transcribed from 子平真诠, 三命通会 and 渊海子平.
  • School comparison: an interactive matrix comparing 12 Daoist schools (正一 · 全真 · 茅山 · 閭山 · 清微 · 神霄 …) across 9 dimensions.
  • Heritage systems: 盲派 (Mang Pai), 古法 (Gufa), 滴天髓, 鬼谷子 and 珞琭子 lineages implemented as distinct engines.

9. Chart-Aware AI Assistant 智能分析 Luận giải bằng AI

The built-in assistant answers open questions about the user's chart with deep-reasoning AI grounded in the computed analysis. Each answer is based on:

  • The full computed chart (40+ sections, ~85 KB of structured data — not a prompt about a star sign).
  • 40+ analysis tools the assistant can invoke (year forecast, marriage timing, career stars, day selection, feng shui directions…).
  • Contextual citation from the 1,523-text library — the assistant can surface the relevant classical text for the user's specific situation.
