🔬 Sample: 阴符经 Yin Fu Jing — 4-Layer Deep Analysis

Layer 1: Essence
Kinh về "đạo cơ" — trời đất vạn vật trộm lẫn nhau; ai nắm thời cơ thì chuyển họa phúc.
Layer 1: Verbatim Chinese
观天之道，执天之行，尽矣。天有五贼，见之者昌。
Layer 3: Logic Thesis
Humans are NOT locked in fixed destiny — heaven/earth/humans all "steal" from each other; whoever grasps the "moment" (机) can change fortune.
Layer 3: Reasoning Chain
天地万物之盗 → humans participate → must see the "opening" → BaZi timing = finding the dao moment → act when window opens → going against = failure
Layer 3: BaZi Practice
Step 1: Find Dụng Thần (needed element). Step 2: Check đại vận (when element arrives). Step 3: Act when window opens. Step 4: Going against = doomed.
Layer 3: Comparative
Differs from 道德经 (passive wu-wei): Yin Fu Jing has MILITARY STRATEGY logic — proactively seizing opportunity. Reason: Yin Fu leans toward Bing Fa (兵法); Dao De Jing leans toward Mệnh Lý (命理).
